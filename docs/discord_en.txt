## Description
Artefacts lying in the world are always visible, like in Shadow of Chernobyl: no detector needed to spot them. A remake of *SoC-style Artefacts (DLTX)* by MaxSpringer as one mod with installer options.

## Features
- **Artefacts always visible** without a detector, from GAMMA, junk, storyline and other mods
- Installer options (Mod Organizer 2), pick what you like:
  - **Keep detector tiers** (recommended): artefacts stay visible, but a detector only beeps for its own tier, like in normal GAMMA
  - **Detectors find all artefacts**: every detector finds every artefact, like the original mod
  - **No glow light**: no coloured light around artefacts, saves GPU near anomaly fields
  - **No idle particles**: no halo, sparks or smoke. Turn this on too if artefacts still look like they glow
- **Enable mod** master switch, MCM configurable, applies immediately. **Debug log** is MCM configurable too
- Lightweight: never touches the game config while you play. One tiny check per ground artefact every 2 s

## Install / remove
- Safe to install or remove mid-playthrough: the mod adds no items and stores nothing in saves. Just load a save
- To change installer options, reinstall the mod in MO2
- Disable the old SoC-style Artefacts (DLTX) mod and all its variants
- Needs DLTX (included in GAMMA), MCM optional. Works on monolith and Quark
