## Description
Artefacts lying in the world are always visible, like in Shadow of Chernobyl: no detector needed to spot them. A remake of *SoC-style Artefacts (DLTX)* by MaxSpringer as one mod.

## Features
- **Artefacts always visible** without a detector
- Covers every artefact: GAMMA's own, junk, storyline and other mods'. No DLTX load order can undo it
- **Artefact glow light** and **Artefact particles** are separate MCM configurable switches. Turn particles off too if artefacts still look like they glow
- **Detectors keep their tiers** (MCM configurable, on by default): artefacts stay visible, but a detector only beeps for its own tier, like in normal GAMMA. Off = every detector finds every artefact
- **Enable mod** master switch and **Debug log**, all MCM configurable
- MCM changes apply after loading a save. Nothing runs per frame (tier mode: one tiny check per ground artefact every 2 s)

## Install / remove
- Safe to install or remove mid-playthrough: the mod adds no items and stores nothing in saves. Just load a save
- Disable the old SoC-style Artefacts (DLTX) mod and all its variants
- Needs DLTX (included in GAMMA), MCM optional. Works on monolith and Quark
